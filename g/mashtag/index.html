Past-conference photo galleries are live on the site, and your team can add to them directly. No developer needed, and nothing else on the site is affected.

Signing in

Go to preview-admin.mashtag.org/admin and sign in with the editor account you were sent. Everything in this guide happens under Content Manager in the left sidebar.

If you’re already signed in to something else on that address, open the link in a private window.

● For 2017–2024

Adding photos to a year that already has a gallery

  1. Open the year

    In the sidebar, choose Yearly Photo Collection, then click the year you want. The photos field shows the photos already in that gallery — one at a time, with arrows to page through them.

  2. Add the new photos

    Click the + on the photo. In the window that opens, drag your photos in, or click Add more assets to pick them from your computer. When they’re all listed, click Finish.

  3. Save, then publish

    Click Save at the top right. Then click Publish. If that button already says Unpublish, the year is live and saving is enough.

    The new photos appear on the site within a minute or two.

● For 2025 and 2026

Setting up a year for the first time

  1. Create the gallery

    Under Yearly Photo Collection, click Create new entry. Type the year into the year box — just the four digits, like 2025. Add the photos the same way as above, then Save and Publish.

  2. Connect it to the conference

    Now go to Yearly Conference and open the same year. Find the photos box, click Add relation, and choose the gallery you just made. Click Save.

    This step is the one to remember. Without it the photos are uploaded but nothing on the site links to them, so the gallery stays invisible.

  3. Check it

    Open mashtag.org/prior-conferences. That year’s card should now have a Photos link alongside Agenda and Abstract Book.

Worth knowing

Send the originals, not resized copies
The site makes its own smaller versions for the grid. Photos narrower than about 500 pixels are skipped and won’t show up at all, so straight-from-the-camera files are the safe choice. Large files are fine.
The order on screen is the order on the page
Photos appear in the order they sit in the photos field. Drag them to rearrange.
Removing a photo doesn’t delete it
Taking a photo out of a year removes it from the website but keeps it in the Media Library, so it can be added back later.
Portrait and landscape both work
The gallery fits each photo to its own shape. Nothing gets cropped, so there’s no need to trim anything to a set size first.
Nothing else on the site is touched
Photo galleries are separate from the agenda, faculty and sponsor pages. Adding photos can’t disturb any of them.
