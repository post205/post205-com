Before you start

Getting in

Head to preview-admin.mashtag.org/admin and sign in with the editor account we sent over. Everything below happens under Content Manager, the first item in the left sidebar.

Already signed into something else at that address? Open it in a private window and it’ll behave.

For 2017 – 2024

Adding to a year that’s already set up

  1. Pick your year

    In the sidebar, choose Yearly Photo Collection and click the year you’re adding to. The photos box shows what’s already there, one photo at a time, with arrows to page through them.

  2. Drop the new ones in

    Click the + sitting on the photo. In the window that opens, drag your photos straight in, or use Add more assets to pick them off your computer. Once they’re all showing, click Finish.

  3. Save, then publish

    Hit Save at the top right, then Publish. If that button already reads Unpublish, the year is live and saving was enough.

    Give it a minute and the photos are on the site. That’s the whole job.

For 2025 and 2026

Starting a brand-new year

Almost the same, with one extra step at the end that’s easy to miss.

  1. Make the gallery

    Under Yearly Photo Collection, click Create new entry. Put the year in the year box — just the four digits, like 2025. Add your photos the same way as above, then Save and Publish.

  2. Point the conference at it

    Now open Yearly Conference and find the same year. In the photos box, click Add relation, pick the gallery you just made, and Save.

    This is the one to remember. Skip it and your photos are safely uploaded, but nothing on the site points to them yet — so the gallery stays hidden and it looks like nothing happened.

  3. Have a look

    Open mashtag.org/prior-conferences. That year’s card should now show a Photos link next to Agenda and Abstract Book. If it’s there, you’re done.

Good to know

A few things worth knowing

Send the originals
The site makes its own smaller copies for the grid, so straight-from-the-camera files are exactly right. Anything narrower than about 500 pixels gets skipped and won’t appear, which is the one way a photo can quietly go missing. Big files are welcome.
The order you see is the order visitors see
Photos appear in the order they sit in the photos box. Drag them around to change it.
Taking a photo out doesn’t delete it
Removing one from a year takes it off the website but keeps it in the Media Library, so you can always put it back.
Portrait and landscape both look right
The gallery fits every photo to its own shape and nothing gets cropped, so there’s no need to trim anything to a set size beforehand.
