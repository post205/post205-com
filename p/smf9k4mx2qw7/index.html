The site served 2.4× the visitors it served the year before. That is not a seasonal bump — it is a level change that happened in July 2025 and has held for thirteen straight months.

The shape of the recovery

Monthly visitors · Mar 2023 – Aug 2026

The averages tell it cleanly. Through the flat period the site averaged 4,023 visitors a month. Over the last twelve months it averaged 11,992 — three times as many. August 2026 is tracking to roughly 10,600, its normal level for the month.

Worth chasing down: nothing in the site's own change log explains the February 2024 drop — no republish, no domain change, no pages deleted that month. The likeliest causes sit outside the site: a search ranking shift, or a change to analytics or tag setup. If your team can tell us what changed on your side in early 2024, we can confirm the cause and make sure it does not repeat.

Twenty-three further crawlers make up the remainder. The site already does the basics right: robots.txt explicitly permits AI reading, and a machine-readable llms.txt summary is live — which is a large part of why ChatGPT reads this site as often as it does.

The gap is what those crawlers find once they arrive. There is no structured data on the homepage — no organisation record, no brand list, no contact block, and no article markup on the fifty-plus nutrition and news stories. AI systems are reading San Miguel Foods in prose and inferring the facts. Companies that publish structured data get quoted with their facts intact.

What stands out

Three observations
Watch

Visitors read 1.68 pages and stop

Across 147,421 visits the site averages under two pages. There are 88 pages live, roughly fifty of them substantial nutrition and corporate stories, and almost nobody reaches a second one. This is a navigation and internal-linking problem, and it is worst on mobile, where nearly half the audience is.

1.68 pages/visit sitewide · 1.49 on iPhone · 1.87 on Windows desktop
Watch

The site was published eight times in twelve months

Eight publishes across the year, with a five-month gap between December 2025 and April 2026. Meanwhile the nutrition content that does exist is genuinely strong — food reformulation, sodium reduction, fortification standards — and it is exactly the material AI answer engines quote. Publishing this rarely leaves the site's strongest asset idle.

Working

The foundations are sound

HTTPS enforced with a valid certificate, GA4 installed, a clean sitemap, page titles and social preview cards present across the site, and llms.txt already published — ahead of most Philippine corporate sites. The recovery since July 2025 is real and it has held for thirteen months.
